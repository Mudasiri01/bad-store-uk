Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know regarding delivery, assembly and our 100-night trial.

We invite you to sleep on your new mattress for at least 30 nights to allow your body and posture to adjust to its support contours. If within 100 nights of delivery you decide it isn't your perfect match, contact our concierges and we will arrange a complimentary collection and either exchange it for an alternative firmness or issue a full refund.

Our logistics service is conducted by a trained 2-person British delivery crew. We phone you 48 hours prior to arrange your 2-hour slot. Upon arrival, we carry the pieces directly into your bedroom of choice (up to the 3rd floor without a lift, or higher with elevator access), unpack everything, and remove all protective transit packaging for recycling.
