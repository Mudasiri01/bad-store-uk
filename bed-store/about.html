Our Story • Founded in London 2012

The Art of British Rest

We believe the bedroom is not simply a room of furniture, but a private sanctuary where the mind and body find restorative equilibrium.

Uncompromising Standards

Centuries of Upholstery Tradition, Engineered for Contemporary Rest.

Kensington & Devon was established with an ambition to challenge throwaway bedroom retail. While mass production turned toward hollow flat-packs and synthetic foams, our craftspeople in Yorkshire and Wiltshire preserved traditional bench-made joinery, hand-tied springs, and organic natural fibres.

Every bedstead we build begins with slow-grown Scandinavian timber, selected for its tight ring density and structural resilience. Every button on our Chesterfield divans is pulled by hand using heavy-gauge waxed twine. The result is furniture that endures gracefully across decades of nightly slumber.

Artisanal Process

The Anatomy of a Kensington & Devon Bed

01

Solid FSC® Framing

Mortise-and-tenon reinforced Scandinavian pine and English ash timbers. Zero flimsy chipboard or cheap staples. Tested to withstand up to 450kg dynamic loads without creaks.

02

Calico Pocket Coils

Up to 5,000 independent responsive British steel coils nested in breathable cotton pockets. Yielding precisely beneath your shoulders while elevating the lumbar spine.

03

Pure Natural Fillings

Fleece wool from Yorkshire Dales, breathable flax linen, organic cotton, and Mongolian cashmere. Chemical-free natural flame resistance meeting UK BS 7177 fire safety.

04

Master Hand-Tufting

Traditional felt rosettes and hand side-stitched borders sewn by our senior seamstresses to prevent mattress edge roll-off and maintain permanent internal alignment.

Conscious Luxury

Rest Easy Knowing Your Bed Leaves a Gentle Footprint.

We reject polyurethane foams that release VOC off-gassing and linger in UK landfills for half a millennium. Instead, we champion natural, circular biodegradable fibres that breathe naturally through the night.

  • Yorkshire Fleece Wool: Naturally thermo-regulating, anti-microbial and resistant to dust mites.
  • AquaClean® Velvets: Stain resistant technology utilizing harmless water rather than harsh toxic chemical sprays.
  • Zero Landfill Mattress Recycling: We dismantle and repurpose old mattresses upon delivering your new sleep system.
