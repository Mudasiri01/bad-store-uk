What to Expect on Delivery Day

Our dedicated 2-person logistics team phones you 48 hours in advance to schedule an exact 2-hour delivery window. On arrival, they will:

  • Inspect room access and staircase clearance.
  • Carry the bedstead or mattress directly to your chosen bedroom (up to 3rd floor).
  • Remove and responsibly recycle all transit polythene and protective cardboard.

Old Bed Recycling Service

During checkout, you can request our eco-disposal service. For a modest £45 contribution, we collect your old bed and mattress and divert 100% of the materials from landfill into agricultural and textile reprocessing.

Artisanal Heritage & Fabric Maintenance

Each Kensington & Devon bed frame is crafted by hand in our workshop using time-honoured mortise and tenon joinery. Here is how to keep your bed in pristine condition for decades to come:

  • Velvet & Bouclé Care: Gently vacuum monthly with a soft brush attachment to lift dust. Blot accidental liquid spills immediately with a clean dry microfiber cloth; do not scrub.
  • Mattress Turning: For dual-sided pocket sprung models, rotate head-to-toe once every two weeks for the first 3 months, then seasonally to allow natural wool comfort layers to settle evenly.
  • Bolts & Glides: Inspect and re-tighten headboard and corner frame bolts annually using the provided allen key to ensure silent stability.
